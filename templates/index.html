Methodology

How CourseMate AI Works

A transparent, five-stage academic synthesis and retrieval pipeline.

1

Input & Upload

Enter any academic topic or upload a lecture document (PDF or TXT) into the workspace.

2

Text Processing

The parser extracts, sanitizes, and partitions text into overlapping semantic passages.

3

Vector Retrieval

TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity identify the most relevant context chunks.

4

Neural Synthesis

The AI engine combines the context with structured prompts to generate academic manuscripts.

5

Revise & Test

Export notes to PDF/Markdown, test your understanding in Quiz Lab, and track progress.
